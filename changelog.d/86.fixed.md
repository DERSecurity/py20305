**Forwarded MirrorUsagePoint exchanges carry the mirrored device's LFDI (#86).**
A MirrorUsagePoint is POSTed to the MirrorUsagePointList and its readings to the
location the server returns, and neither path names an EndDevice, so every
device's meter readings were forwarded under the client certificate's LFDI. A
MirrorUsagePoint whose `deviceLFDI` names a device registered with
`update_device_lfdi_prefixes` is now forwarded under that LFDI, and so is every
readings POST to its location, including after rediscovery. A `deviceLFDI` that
names no registered device keeps the client certificate's LFDI.
