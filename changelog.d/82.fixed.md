**The SunSpec connector converts model 701 status points to IEEE 2030.5 codes (#82).**
`InvSt`, `St`, `ConnSt` and `Alrm` were copied into DERStatus as read, but the
enumerations differ: a running inverter was reported as `inverterStatus` 3
(starting) and an inverter that was on as `operationalModeStatus` 1 (off).
`inverterStatus` is now `InvSt` + 1, `operationalModeStatus` is 1 for OFF and 2
for ON, `connectStatus` is the 2030.5-2023 bitmap (connected, energized) and
`genConnectStatus` the 2030.5-2018 bitmap (connected, available, operating,
fault), and `alarmStatus` carries only the model 701 alarms that DERStatus also
names (AC over- and under-voltage, over- and under-frequency). `build_der_status`
now sends a 2018 server the `genConnectStatus` a connector supplies, and
`connectStatus` otherwise. The print connector reports a running device
(`inverterStatus` 4, `operationalModeStatus` 2).
