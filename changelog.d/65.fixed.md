**A cleared charge-rate limit no longer stays on a SunSpec device (#65).** When
the last of `opModLoadLimW` and `opModMaxLimWAbsorb` clears, the connector writes
back the `WChaRteMax` the device held before the first one, as IEEE 2030.5-2018
10.10.4.2, CSIP 4.4.2 and CSIP-AUS 4.4 require of a mode the DefaultDERControl
does not carry. Before, the absorb limit left its value in force after the event.
The discharge-rate setting `opModMaxLimWInject` writes is not restored yet (#66).
