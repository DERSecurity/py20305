**The SunSpec connector enforces the CSIP-AUS load limit, opModLoadLimW, on
storage devices (#65).** It was accepted and silently ignored. The limit is the
maximum charge rate, written in watts to model 702 `WChaRteMax`; when
`opModMaxLimWAbsorb` is active too, the lower of the two is written. Clearing
writes nothing unless the other limit is still active, so after an event the
DefaultDERControl sets the next value. A device that does not declare
charge-rate limiting reports the control as not supported (251).
