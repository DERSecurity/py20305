**The price stream relays an interval that ends with no interval after it (#127).** When the active TimeTariffInterval ends, or is cancelled, and none follows, connectors' `notification_price` now receives it with transition `completed` (its effective end has passed) or `cancelled`, as the control stream does. Before, nothing was relayed and a connector kept the expired price through the gap. A connector that ignores `transition` re-applies the ended price, so it behaves as before.
