**The SunSpec Modbus connector and its representations are removed (#87).**
py20305 keeps to IEEE 2030.5; converting a device protocol's points to IEEE
2030.5 values is the application's job. Removed, with no replacement in the
package:

- `py20305.connectors.sunspec` (`ConnectorSunSpec`) and
  `py20305.connectors.sunspec_core` (`SunSpecModbusConnector`, the SunSpec meter
  reader and its power-sign calibration);
- `py20305.connectors.translation` (`translate_to_sunspec` and the SunSpec
  point-path mapping) and its re-export from `py20305.connectors`;
- the `sunspec_connector_points.txt` points list;
- the `sunspec` device type and `SunSpecDeviceConfig`, with its meter-only
  `power_sign` setting;
- the `sunspec` extra (pysunspec2, pyserial), which `all` and the container
  image no longer include.

An application that drove a SunSpec device through the package carries its
own connector: a `BaseConnector` subclass configured as a `custom` device or
supplied through a `factory_resolver`. The registry and device roles (`role`,
`revenue_quality`, `mirror_under`) are unchanged; the connector interface
changes as #85 describes.
