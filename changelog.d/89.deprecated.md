**`server.server_2018_compat` is deprecated (#89).**
`true` means `server.schema.base: 2018` plus `server.missing_location_fallback:
true`, and `false` means `server.schema.base: 2023`. Setting it raises a
`DeprecationWarning` and logs a warning that names the replacement. An explicit
`server.schema.base` wins over it. It is kept for one release line.
