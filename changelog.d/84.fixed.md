**A phased system voltage or current is registered under its own mRID (#84).**
Under the default reading profile, a connector that gives the system voltage
or current a phase code with `ReadingOverride(phase=...)` has it registered at
slot 63 (voltage) or 64 (current) rather than slot 4 or 7, which were
registered without a phase: IEEE 2030.5-2023 §10.11.3 Rule n forbids changing
a reading's ReadingType once created. A connector that sets no phase, or phase
0, keeps slots 4 and 7. A connector that already set a phase on the system
voltage has it moved to slot 63; a server keeps the old slot 4 MeterReading
until it removes it. Also under the default profile, a reading that would post
under a slot the MirrorUsagePoint did not register (a per-line block, an energy
total or a phased reading first reported after registration) now re-POSTs the
MirrorUsagePoint first, and is held back while that registration is refused,
instead of meeting a 400 (Rule h.3).
