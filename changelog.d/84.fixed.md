**The system voltage and current readings carry a phase code (#84).** Under the
default reading profile, a DER that reports `ACType` posts its system voltage,
the line-to-line average, with the line-to-line phase code for its wiring (132
AB for single-phase and split-phase, 224 ABC for three-phase), and a DER or
meter that reports `ACType` posts its system current with the wiring's phase
code (128 A, 132 AB or 224 ABC). A meter's voltage keeps no phase code, since a
SunSpec meter reports the line-to-neutral average where it has one.
Without a phase, a server could not tell the line-to-line voltage from a
line-to-neutral one. IEEE 2030.5-2023 §10.11.3 Rule n forbids changing a
reading's ReadingType once created, so the two readings move to new mRIDs
(slots 63 and 64) and the old ones (slots 4 and 7) are no longer posted for such
a device. A server keeps the old MeterReadings until it removes them. A device
without `ACType`, and the signed load convention profile, are unchanged. A cycle
that omits `ACType` now uses the one the MirrorUsagePoint was registered with
under both profiles. Under the default profile, a reading that would post under
a slot the MirrorUsagePoint did not register (`ACType` or an energy total first
reported after registration) now re-POSTs the MirrorUsagePoint first, and is
held back while that registration is refused, instead of meeting a 400 (Rule
h.3).
